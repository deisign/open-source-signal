## Scope limits

No backdating or modifications to historical issues. A review is publication history, not proof of current maintenance, security or operational fitness. Generic platforms mentioned as a reporting surface, vendor method articles and unavailable military systems were not automatically promoted into tools. A structured inventory of every review and explicit-name mention is in `data/tool_publication_audit.json`. The existing 24 October Tool Radar reserve remains separate.
